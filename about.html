I'm Blake, a tattoo artist in Toronto with ten years behind the machine. I trained in design at OCAD University, and the drawing still starts there: on paper, built around your body and what you want to carry.

Outside the studio I work in metal, ceramics, wood, beads and fabric. All of it feeds back into the tattoos. You'll see it on the blog.

I run my own bookings, so when you write in, you're talking to me.

Aftercare

how to heal it right
First 24 hours

Leave it alone

Keep the original bandage on for as long as I tell you. When you remove it, wash gently with your hands and fragrance-free soap, then pat dry.

Days 2–14

Thin layers, twice a day

A thin layer of fragrance-free, dye-free lotion in the morning and evening. If it looks shiny, you've used too much — tattoos heal better slightly dry.

Whole healing

No soaking, no sun

No pools, lakes, baths or saunas until fully healed. Keep it out of direct sun, and once healed, sunscreen keeps the linework sharp for years.

While it peels

Don't pick, don't pull

It will itch and flake — that's normal. Picking pulls ink out and leaves patchy spots. Loose, breathable clothing over the area helps most.

If something looks wrong — spreading redness, heat, swelling, pus or fever — that's outside normal healing. Message me and see a doctor; don't wait it out.
